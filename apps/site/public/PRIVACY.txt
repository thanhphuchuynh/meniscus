Privacy note for the meniscus demo site

The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser's localStorage
under the key "meniscus-theme". You can remove it using your browser's site-data
controls. The preference is not sent to the site by its application code.

The current site source does not include analytics, advertising trackers,
accounts, or forms that submit personal information. When you load the site,
your browser requests pages, fonts, and images from the hosting service. That
service may keep standard request logs under its own configuration. The site
owner should review the hosting provider's logging and retention settings
before deployment and update this note if those settings or the site change.

The optional glass sound is synthesized in your browser and starts disabled.
The "Edit in StackBlitz" button sends the public example source files to
StackBlitz only when clicked. StackBlitz opens separately and has its own
privacy policy. No account details or personal input are included by this site.
